# Cold Email vs. LinkedIn Outreach: Where Should a B2B Founder Spend 30 Minutes a Day?

*The debate every early-stage B2B SaaS founder runs into. Here's the honest math, and what we'd do with 30 minutes daily.*

You're at zero paying customers. You have maybe 30 minutes a day for outreach. Cold email or LinkedIn?

It's the wrong question — but the answer still matters, because most founders pick one channel, go all-in, and quit both when neither delivers in two weeks. Here's how we think about it.

---

## What each channel actually is

**Cold email** is a *sales* channel. It's direct, measurable, and fast to test. You write a targeted email, it lands in a decision-maker's inbox, and you get a reply rate you can optimize week to week.

**LinkedIn** is a *relationship* channel. It's slower, compounding, and only works once you have something to compound — a network, a profile that looks credible, a history of useful comments.

That distinction changes what you should do with your first 30 minutes.

---

## The 30-minute daily split we'd run

**20 minutes: cold email, properly done**

- 5 targeted, researched emails a day (not 50 blasts)
- Every email references something specific about the prospect's company or recent activity
- Short — 90-120 words, one question, one reason to reply
- Verified addresses, warmed-up domain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC in place before you send a single one

This is the channel where a first customer is most likely to come from. The math: 5 emails × 20 business days = 100 tailored touches a month. At even a 2% reply rate, that's 2 real conversations — and conversations are what convert at zero budget.

**10 minutes: LinkedIn, done as a trust layer**

- 5 genuine comments on posts from founders at companies you'd love as customers
- No pitching in the comments. Add a specific observation or a useful resource
- Keep your profile current: clear headline, what you're building, one good pinned post

You're not expecting a sale from these comments. You're building the profile that makes your eventual outreach *not feel cold*. When you do send a connection request, it's warm.

---

## What we learned the hard way

1. **14 cold emails is not a test.** Most founders quit after sending single digits. You can't judge a channel on 14 sends. Judge it on 100 tailored, delivered, tracked emails.
2. **X/Twitter is not a distribution channel at small scale.** ~400 followers feels like momentum but drives few first customers. Treat it as credibility, not acquisition.
3. **LinkedIn with 0 connections is a cold start.** Posting alone goes nowhere until you build a network — which takes weeks of consistent commenting. It's a second-order channel.
4. **Directories are a drip, not a spike.** Submit once, move on.

---

## The verdict

If you have 30 minutes a day and zero customers: **spend 20 on cold email, 10 on LinkedIn comments.** Reassess after 3 weeks of doing both consistently — not after 3 days of one.

Cold email gets you the first conversations. LinkedIn makes everything after those conversations warmer. Neither replaces the other.
