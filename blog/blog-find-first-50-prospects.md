# How to Find Your First 50 Cold Email Prospects (Without Buying a List)

*You don't need a $300 email list to start cold outreach. You need 50 companies that look like your best customers — and they're findable for free in an afternoon.*

The #1 excuse we hear from founders who "tried cold email": *"I don't know who to email."* The #2 excuse: *"I don't have a list."* Both are solvable in a single focused afternoon. Here's the exact process we use.

---

## Step 1: Define the "lookalike" in one sentence

Before you search, you need a filter. Not a vague ICP ("SaaS companies") — a sentence that lets you say yes/no in five seconds:

> "B2B SaaS companies with 10-200 employees, founder-led sales, that sell a tool to other businesses (not consumers), headquartered in the US or Europe."

Write down 5 companies you *already know* fit this. They're your seed list and your compass for finding the other 45.

## Step 2: Mine your own backyard (free)

- **Your competitors' customers**: Go to your 5 competitors' sites. Find their "customers" or "case studies" pages. Every logo there is a warm lead — they already pay for a tool like yours.
- **Job boards**: Search "hiring [role] at [company]" — a company posting for a Sales or Growth role is actively spending on growth tools.
- **Recent funding/founding announcements**: Companies that just raised or launched a new product have budget and a reason to buy.

## Step 3: Use directories that are actually free

- **BuiltWith / SimilarWeb (free tiers)**: Pick a competitor's domain, see what tools they use and who else uses them.
- **Crunchbase (free tier)**: Filter by industry, location, funding round, employee count. Export a shortlist.
- **G2 / Capterra (free)**: Browse the "alternatives" pages of your category. Every alternative listing is a company actively evaluating tools like yours — *right now*.

## Step 4: Find the right person at each company

You don't email "info@". You email a human. For each of your 50 companies:

1. **Who decides?** For a $29-99/mo tool: the founder (most likely), or the head of sales/marketing if the company is bigger.
2. **Find their email** with a free verifier or a quick search (`firstname@company.com` patterns, or tools like Hunter's free tier).
3. **Verify it bounces?** Never send blind. A 2%+ bounce rate poisons your domain.

## Step 5: Score your list, send the top 20 first

Rank the 50 by "how obviously they'd say yes":

- They already use a tool like yours (10 pts)
- They're actively hiring for growth (5 pts)
- They just launched/raised (5 pts)
- You can reference something specific (10 pts)

Send the top 20 first. Not the whole list. Quality of targeting — not volume — is what determines reply rate.

---

## The free-stack checklist

- [ ] 5 seed companies you already know
- [ ] Competitors' customer logos mined (free)
- [ ] 20-30 names from free directory tiers
- [ ] Decision-maker identified per company
- [ ] Emails verified, bounce risk <2%
- [ ] Score sorted, top 20 queued first
